### About BTelling
**BTelling** is an independent Minecraft studio creating immersive narrative, horror, and custom-mechanic experiences for **Minecraft Java & Bedrock Edition**. We focus on atmosphere, custom 3D models, original UI, voiceovers, soundtracks, and scripting.

---

### Featured Project

#### [No, I'm not a human | Map & Add-on](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft-bedrock/maps/no-im-not-a-human-map)
A psychological survival-horror experience for **Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.50+** inspired by *No, I'm not a Human*. The sun has turned lethal, and mysterious entities called **"Visitors"** knock on your door at night pretending to be ordinary people.

- **14-Day Campaign** with daily TV broadcasts, unexpected events & multiple endings
- **Custom 3D Furniture & Interactions** (door peephole, window blinds, TV, radio, phone, bathtub)
- **Unique 3D Guests** with custom dialogues, inspection mechanics & hidden secrets
- **Original UI, Custom Voiceovers & Soundtrack**
- **Solo & Co-op Multiplayer** support
